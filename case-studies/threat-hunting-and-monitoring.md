# Threat Hunting and Security Monitoring

## Objective

Explore complementary detection layers and translate observable indicators into reusable detection logic.

## Tools and outcomes

- Developed and tested YARA rules against controlled samples.
- Reviewed strings, conditions, and false-positive risk.
- Examined network-level events with Snort and Security Onion.
- Used Sigma as a vendor-neutral format for expressing log-detection logic.
- Compared where file, network, and log detections provide complementary visibility.

The work reinforced a layered-detection mindset and the importance of validating rules before operational use.
